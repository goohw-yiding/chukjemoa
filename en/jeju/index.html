Split the island east and west

Jeju is a small island, but driving across it eats your day. Locals split it in two and so should you — one day east, one day west.
East (조천읍 · 구좌읍 · 성산읍 · 표선면 · 남원읍) is the sunrise side: Seongsan Ilchulbong, Seopjikoji, Woljeongri beach.
West (애월읍 · 한림읍 · 한경면 · 대정읍 · 안덕면) is the sunset side: the Aewol coastal cafés, Hyeopjae beach, Sanbangsan.
