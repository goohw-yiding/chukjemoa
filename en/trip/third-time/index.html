What this page does not know

  • Opening hours, closing days and ticket prices are not in our data. Check before you go.
  • Rankings come from the number of registered attractions, so a city famous for one single place (Jeonju Hanok Village, Andong Hahoe Village) ranks lower than it deserves.
  • The crowd multiplier describes the district, not any single place inside it.
  • Foreign-visitor data covers only the top 40 districts. "Not ranked" means "not in that 40" — not zero.

Korean names are shown next to the romanised name on purpose — show your screen to a taxi driver or ticket clerk.
